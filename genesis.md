## The story of

# GENESIS

## as known by Science

---

In the beginning there was
<a class="note" title="[literally, 'no thing']">nothing</a>,
and everything was
[one](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gravitational_singularity):
eternal and without measure.

<a class="note" title="[a reverent exclamation; awesomely, unexpectedly]"><em>Then,</em></a> the
[universe](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Universe)
[exploded](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Big_Bang) into being,
[forming](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nucleosynthesis)
the [first element](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hydrogen "Hydrogen")
and the [second element](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Helium "Helium")
<a class="note" title="[omitted in some versions of the text]">in the proportion of three to one</a>,
creating the billions of
[galaxies and nebulae](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Galaxy_formation_and_evolution);
and from [thirteen eons ago](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Age_of_the_universe "an eon is one billion years")
comes the light from billions of
[ancestral suns](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Population_III "Population III stars").

The ancestral suns
[burned the first element](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Proton-proton_chain)
to form the second element and the
[third element](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lithium "Lithium")
and so on down to the
[twenty-sixth element](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Iron-56 "Iron");
and the ancestral suns
[collapsed](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gravitational_collapse)
each in their time, and
[exploded in every direction](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Supernova),
giving birth with their remains to billions of
[elder suns](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Population_II#Population_II_stars "Population II stars").

And the elder suns, having burned for several eons, collapsed each in their time, and exploded in every direction.  So the heavens are filled with
[stars](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stars), including the
[Sun](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sun): and they have
burned for several eons and will burn for several more.

So the
[ninety-two elements](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Supernova_nucleosynthesis)
were created;
and they [gathered to form the planets](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Planet#Formation), including the
[Earth](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Earth) which came to
[orbit](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Orbit) the Sun;
and also the moons which came to orbit the planets, including one
[Moon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Moon)
which came to orbit the Earth.

The Earth
[spun](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Earth%27s_rotation)
night and day and night again, and the rock and ocean and atmosphere
[were separate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Planetary_differentiation)
by [four eons ago](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Age_of_the_Earth);
and [volcanoes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Volcano)
made the [mountains](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mountain);
and [rivers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/River) carved the
[canyons](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Canyon); and the Moon
brought the [tides](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tides);
and the wind blew and the rain fell without question.

<a class="note"><em>Then,</em></a>
in the ocean [there
came to be](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Origin_of_life) a form of
[proto-life](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/RNA_world_hypothesis),
which was
[reproduction](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Biological_reproduction);
reproduction begat
[evolution](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Evolution);
evolution begat
[metabolism](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Metabolism);
and by four eons ago there were
[simple cells](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Prokaryote).

Some simple cells became
[proto-plants](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cyanobacteria "Cyanobacteria"),
which [ate light from the sun](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Photosynthesis);
but their
[waste accumulated and became poison](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Oxygen_Catastrophe)
to them.  And so some simple cells became
[aerobia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aerobic_organism),
which ate the bodies and breathed the waste; and by two eons ago the
proto-plants and aerobia had come to equilibrium in the oceans, and the
[eighth element](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Oxygen "Oxygen")
had come to
[equilibrium](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Oxygen_cycle "21%")
in the atmosphere.

The simple cells and proto-plants and aerobia evolved independently;
and by two eons ago
they had [merged](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Symbiogenesis)
into [compound cells](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Eukaryote),
which eat light from the Sun, and also flesh of the earth.

Some compound cells
[became joined](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Evolution_of_multicellularity)
and formed [coral](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Coral)
and
[fungus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fungus);
and one cell brought forth many cells that
[grew differently](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Differentiated_cell),
yet [functioned as one](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Multicellular_organism).
And by one eon ago, vegetation had covered the earth.

Vegetation became
[plants yielding seed](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Spermatophyte),
and [trees](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tree) bearing
[fruit](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fruit) in which is their seed.
And there came to be [fish](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fish)
in the ocean, and all manner of
[animals](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Animals)
along the ground.
Then came [flowers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Angiosperms) feeding
[insects](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Insect) which
[spread their seed](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Co-evolution),
and [birds](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Birds) in the air;
the stronger eats the weaker and the weakest
eats from the sun, and it is equilibrium.

<a class="note"><em>Then,</em></a> it came to pass that
[*Australopithecus*](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Australopithecus),
which walked upright on two feet, begat
[*Homo habilis*](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Homo_habilis),
which made use of tools; *habilis* begat
[*Homo erectus*](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Homo_erectus),
which captured fire; and *erectus* begat
[*Homo sapiens*](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Homo_sapiens),
which moved their tongues in speech.

Now it is the end of eons; and *Homo sapiens* is man and woman.
